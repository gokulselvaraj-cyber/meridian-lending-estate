Help with your application

What you will need

  • Your address and contact details
  • An idea of your credit score
  • The value of the property and how much you want to borrow

The steps

  1. About you: your name, address and credit score.
  2. The property: we show you an indicative rate straight away.
  3. Check and submit: review what you told us and send it.

The rate on the property step is a guide. Your final rate is set when we check your credit file.

Your sign-in account

Your portal account is how you sign in to this website. It is separate from your loan account number, which appears on your statements.
