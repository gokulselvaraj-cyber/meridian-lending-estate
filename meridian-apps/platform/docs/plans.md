# Product plans and broker terms

## Plans

| Plan | What it adds |
|---|---|
| STANDARD | Nothing extra |
| PLUS | The first late fee in each calendar year is waived |

## Broker commission

Paid when a loan a broker introduced is disbursed:

- **0.50%** of the loan amount for loans below 250,000
- **0.40%** for loans of 250,000 or more
